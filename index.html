About

The Miami Heat compete in the NBA's Eastern Conference and are known for their championship history and incredible culture. With Giannis Antetokounmpo now in Miami, the Heat have entered a new era alongside longtime star Bam Adebayo. Giannis wears No. 7 for Miami, while Bam continues to wear No. 13.

Top Athletes

Dwyane Wade

Known as "D-Wade" or "Flash," he became one of basketball's elite guards and led the Heat to championships in 2006, 2012 and 2013. Wade retired in 2019 as one of the greatest players in Miami Heat history.

↑

Miami Heat Big Three

LeBron James, Dwyane Wade and Chris Bosh formed Miami's legendary Big Three from 2010 to 2014, winning two NBA championships and reaching four straight NBA Finals.

↑

Miami Heat Currently

Miami's current era is led by Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo. Giannis brings two MVP awards and elite two-way play to Miami, while Bam provides All-Star-level defense, rebounding and versatility. Together, they give the Heat a powerful new foundation.

Giannis Bam

Bam Adebayo

Bam has been a cornerstone of the Heat since 2017. He is known for his switchable defense, rebounding, passing and ability to impact the game on both ends of the floor.
