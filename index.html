About

The Miami Heat compete in the National Basketball Association (NBA) as a member of the Southeast Division of the Eastern Conference. The club plays its home games at Kaseya Center and has won three NBA championships. The franchise began play in the 1988–89 season as an expansion team. Their culture is amazing — it's the best in the league.

Giannis Antetokounmpo is one of the newest stars to join the Miami Heat, bringing his all-around game, size, athleticism, and championship experience to Miami.

Top Athletes

Dwyane Wade

Known as "D-Wade" or "Flash," he became one of pro basketball's elite guards, leading the Heat to championships in 2006, 2012 and 2013. Wade retired in 2019 as the team's all-time leader in numerous categories.

Miami Heat Big Three

The Big Three were LeBron James, Dwyane Wade, and Chris Bosh, who played together in Miami from 2010–11 through 2013–14. They won two championships and reached four straight NBA Finals.

Miami Heat Currently

Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo give Miami two elite two-way stars. Giannis brings his MVP-level scoring, playmaking, rebounding and defense, while Bam provides versatile defense, rebounding, playmaking and scoring from the frontcourt.
